STEP 1

🗺️ Random position

📖 How does it work?

The pick random position block works like rolling a die onto the arena map. Every time, the computer picks a different place — that’s why the colored blocks never appear in the same spot!

Imagine closing your eyes and throwing a ball onto the arena floor. You never know where it will land. That’s exactly how a random position works. 🎯

🧪 Experiment

Click the button and watch the blocks appear in new places every time!

STEP 2

🎲 The random variable

📖 How does it work?

The roll variable is like a magic die that keeps rolling a number: 1 or 2.

When it rolls 1 — the colored blocks are traps 💀
When it rolls 2 — the colored blocks are rewards 🎁

The faster it changes, the harder it is to predict what comes next!

STEP 3

🎁 Rewards and traps

📖 How does it work?

Every colored block in the arena has two faces: it can be a reward 🎁 or a trap 💀.

What happens is decided by the roll variable from step 2. When the number changes — all the blocks change along with it! You never know whether the block under your feet is helping you or hurting you right now.

BONUS

🤖 Fun facts about randomness

🎲

A computer actually can’t be truly random! It uses special math formulas whose results only look random — we call this pseudorandomness.

🤖

Artificial intelligence (AI) learns through random trying. Just like you learn a game by trial and error, AI randomly tries different answers and remembers the best ones! 🧠

🌧️

Weather forecasts use randomness too! Scientists use computers to randomly simulate thousands of versions of the weather and check which one is the most likely. ☀️⛈️

🎮

In Minecraft, every new world is created from a random “seed”. It’s a single number that the whole world grows from — mountains, caves and oceans! 🏔️

🐒

There’s a famous saying: if a monkey pressed random keys for long enough, it would eventually write a whole book! Of course, it would take billions of years 😄

🔒

Randomness protects our passwords on the internet! When you log in to a website, the computer uses random numbers to encrypt your data so that no outsider can read it. 🛡️

🦎

Animals use randomness to survive! A hare escapes from a fox in a zigzag — it randomly turns left or right so the predator can’t guess where it will run. Clever! 🐇💨

❄️

Every snowflake has a random shape! When water freezes in a cloud, ice crystals grow in random directions. That’s why no two snowflakes in the whole world are the same! 🌨️
